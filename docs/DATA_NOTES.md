# Data notes

- **Main source:** [Saisirishan Indian vehicle dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/saisirishan/indian-vehicle-dataset). Its archive has 1,697 image/XML pairs; two XMLs point to missing images, leaving 1,695 usable pairs. Kaggle metadata reports CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Source images and generated crops stay local and Git-ignored.
- **Source mix:** 601 State-wise OLX, 440 Google images, 654 video frames. A 180-crop visual sample found eight clear issues: four foreign plates, one vehicle badge, and three incomplete text labels. Three more crops need closer inspection. XML texts remain largely unverified; do not describe the set as clean Indian ground truth.
- **Datacluster sample:** 47 images and 25 labeled plate instances. Its local `datacluster/README.md` says CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and evaluation-only use. The 25 plates are a small cross-source check, not a reliable population estimate.
- **Split:** 847 train, 339 calibration, 255 validation, and 254 test images from the Kaggle source. Repeated normalized plate text and frames sharing a source-video key are grouped before splitting. `splits_v2.json` is generated locally. Similar but differently labeled images may still exist; a perceptual duplicate audit is future work.
- **Label status:** `data/labels/kaggle_xml_labels.csv` was generated from XML object names by `scripts/prepare_indian.py`; these entries are **not human-reviewed labels**. The review tool writes `data/labels/kaggle_labels.csv` separately, and reviewed entries override XML text when loaded. `results/audit_*.jpg` records a 180-crop sampled inspection and `results/audit_issues.csv` records clear and suspected problems. Do not mistake generated labels for verified corrections.
- **Protected results:** `results/final_test.json` and `results/end_to_end_test.json` now exist locally and refuse ordinary overwrites. Their methods and limitations are in [EVALUATION_REPORT.md](EVALUATION_REPORT.md).
